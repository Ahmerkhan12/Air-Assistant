Simple 4-Step Setup

How to Install on Android

Since AirAssistant is distributed independently as a direct APK, follow these quick steps to get started in under 60 seconds.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download APK button above on your Android phone (or scan the QR code from your computer).

File: AirAssistant-v1.0.0.apk (~58 MB)
2

Allow Install from Unknown Sources

When you open the downloaded file, Android may display a prompt saying "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source".

Tap Settings → Toggle Allow from this source → Tap Install.
3

Open & Grant Permissions

Open AirAssistant. Grant the two straightforward permissions it asks for:

  • Nearby Devices (Bluetooth): To detect your connected AirPods.
  • Accessibility Service: To intercept double-taps while Spotify/music is playing.
4

Put on AirPods & Double Tap!

Pop in your AirPods, double-tap or squeeze the earbud stem, and speak to Google Gemini or Assistant immediately!
